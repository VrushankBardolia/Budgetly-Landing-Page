Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026 · Last updated: August 30, 2026

Plain-language notice: Budgetly is an independent, solo-built app developed and maintained by Vrushank Bardolia. We respect your financial privacy—Budgetly does not request bank credentials, sell your data, or serve third-party ads.

1. Overview

Budgetly is built and maintained by Vrushank Bardolia ("Budgetly," "we," "us," "our," or "the developer"), an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Budgetly Android application, how it is protected, and your rights regarding your data. Read this alongside our Terms & Conditions.

2. Data we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive basic authentication details (name, email address, and profile photo) from your Google account via Firebase Authentication. We never receive or store your Google password.

Financial data you enter

Transactions, amounts, category names, notes, budget targets, and custom sheets that you manually enter into the app. Budgetly does not link to bank accounts or credit cards—all financial data is manually logged by you.

Device & diagnostics

Basic technical diagnostics such as operating system version, app version, and crash logs collected via Google Firebase services to keep the app stable and bug-free.

Biometric data

If you enable the biometric app lock, authentication (fingerprint or Face Unlock) is handled entirely by your device's operating system. Budgetly never receives, accesses, or stores your biometric data.

3. How we use your data

  • To authenticate your account and sync your expense records securely across your devices;
  • To calculate monthly totals, daily safe-to-spend limits, category breakdowns, and PDF exports;
  • To deliver local reminders you have scheduled;
  • To diagnose app crashes and fix technical issues;
  • To protect the app against unauthorized access.

Your financial data is never used for advertising, marketing, or profiling. There are zero ads in Budgetly.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data to fulfill our service agreement (providing the expense tracking features you sign up for) and based on your consent (e.g. enabling local reminders or biometric app locks).

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is processed only through essential infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — for secure user authentication and cloud database sync.

6. Data storage & security

Your account data is stored in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, protected by industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Additionally, on your Android device, you can turn on Biometric Lock for an extra layer of privacy.

7. Biometric app lock

The optional biometric lock is executed locally on-device through Android system APIs. Budgetly only receives a pass/fail verification signal from the OS.

8. Data retention

Your expense records are retained while your account remains active so you can access your spending history. If you request account deletion, your data is permanently deleted from our database within 30 days.

9. Your rights & choices

You have full control over your data. You can:

  • Add, edit, or delete any transaction or category directly within the app;
  • Export your monthly expense reports as PDF files anytime;
  • Delete your account and data permanently from Settings;
  • Disable local daily reminders at any time in app settings.

For any privacy requests or inquiries, reach out to vrushankbardolia.dev@gmail.com.

10. Children's privacy

Budgetly is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. International transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on secure Google Cloud servers located in global datacenters equipped with standard data protection protocols.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when revisions take effect.

13. Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please email vrushankbardolia.dev@gmail.com.
