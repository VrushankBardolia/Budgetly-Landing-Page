Questions

Good to know

Is Budgetly free?

Yes, all of it. No trial, no paywall, no "premium" tier waiting behind a paywall. I built this for myself first.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's a one-person project and Play Store listings take time and money I'd rather spend building. The APK on GitHub is always the latest version.

Is it safe to sideload?

The APK is built and published by me, straight from this GitHub repo — nothing else touches it. You can check the source yourself before installing if you'd like.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Your data lives in your account, not just on the device. Sign in again with Google on any phone to pick up where you left off.

Does Budgetly track or share my financial data?

Never. Budgetly does not connect to your bank accounts, sell your data, or show third-party ads. Everything you log stays completely private.

Can I export my monthly spending reports?

Yes! You can export your monthly expense summaries or custom sheet records as clean, printable PDF statements anytime from the app.

How does the daily safe-to-spend limit work?

Budgetly divides your remaining monthly budget by the number of days left in the month, automatically recalculating whenever you log a new expense.

Will my data stay safe when a new build is released?

Yes! Your data is securely synced to your Google account via Firebase, so installing new APK updates will never erase your spending history.
